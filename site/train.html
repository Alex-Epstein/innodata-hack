02 · RL environments

Train the model by trial and reward

Each task ships with a verifier, a small program that reads the model's answer and returns a reward: 1 if correct, 0 if not (envs.jsonl + verifier.py). The model tries thousands of tasks, is rewarded for right answers, and learns the skill.

The reward can't be faked or argued with, because it's computed from the data, and our tasks sit where the model sometimes fails, which is where training teaches the most.
